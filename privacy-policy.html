Jam Tracks Hub Privacy & User Content

Effective date: August 27, 2026

What the extension does

Jam Tracks Hub Song Key Finder estimates the musical key of an audio file or a YouTube video selected by the user.

Data processed

When you choose an audio file, the extension sends that file to https://jamtrackshub.com for musical-key analysis. When you submit a YouTube link, the extension sends that URL to the same service so its audio can be retrieved and analyzed.

The extension may read the URL of the currently active browser tab only when you open the extension, so it can prefill the form when that tab is a YouTube video.

Storage and retention

Uploaded and downloaded audio is stored temporarily while the analysis job runs and is deleted when processing finishes. The service may retain the submitted YouTube URL and analysis result in operational analysis history for reliability and troubleshooting.

Recent results and interface preferences are stored locally in your browser. You can clear recent results from the extension.

If you join the email list, your email address is stored so Jam Tracks Hub can send updates about new backing tracks and related site news. You may request removal by contacting the site owner.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide musical-key analysis, maintain service reliability, send requested email updates, and troubleshoot failures. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to determine creditworthiness.

Sharing and security

Data is processed by the hosting and infrastructure providers required to operate the analysis service. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect data in transit and during temporary processing.

Your choices

You may use file upload or YouTube analysis only when you choose to do so. You can clear locally stored analysis history at any time or uninstall the extension to remove its local data.

Song Workspace and local storage

Song Workspace processes song titles, lyrics, chords, ChordPro text, and Jam Tracks Hub Song Documents locally in your browser. This song content is not uploaded to Jam Tracks Hub.

Song Workspace does not provide cloud song storage or browser-to-browser synchronization. Clearing browser or site data may remove locally stored songs, so use Backup All or export files when you need a portable copy.

Exported files may contain lyrics or other content you entered. Use or share them only where you have the necessary rights or legal permission.

User-provided content and copyright

Jam Tracks Hub provides tools for users to work with content they provide. Song Workspace is not intended to create a Jam Tracks Hub-hosted lyrics library.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have the rights or legal permission necessary for content they import, use, export, or share.

Whether a particular use is permitted depends on the content and applicable law; local processing alone does not make a use lawful.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support contact shown on the Chrome Web Store listing.
