How BPM affects auto-scroll

Auto-scroll uses BPM as its starting pace, so a higher BPM generally moves the chart faster. Zoom and line spacing change the chart height, while song length, section height, and screen height change how far there is to scroll. Use Scroll Speed to fine-tune the feel.
